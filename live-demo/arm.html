🦾 SO-101 Digital Twin

A 3D replica of the SO-101 arm. Move the six joint dials and the on-screen arm bends in real time — and if a real arm is connected, it copies every motion. Flip to Mirror mode, turn torque off, pose the physical arm by hand, and watch the twin follow. Joint ranges are the real ones from the SO-ARM101 URDF.
